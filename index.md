I am currently Director of <a href="https://data.4tu.nl/" target="_blank">4TU.ResearchData</a>, a research infrastructure, community and training provider based in the Netherlands. Our main service is a trusted digital repository which supports Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable (FAIR) data and software by offering curation, sharing, long-term access and preservation. 4TU.ResearchData is led and governed by the four technical universities in the Netherlands: Delft University of Technology, Eindhoven University of Technology, University of Twente and Wageningen University & Research.

I have a background in open science and research data across a range of national and institutional contexts, including working in Ireland on national policy and coordination as National Open Research Coordinator, and positions in Germany and Australia supporting open science in European projects and academic libraries. 

My research background is in musicology, digital humanities and information management.
